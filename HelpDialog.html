📄 DocuPDF Sign

Signs existing PDFs (invoices, MOUs) by overlaying signer signatures and an audit stamp, then writes one-time sign links back into your sheet.

How a signature happens

  • Mark a row Signing Required = Yes and make sure it has a PDF (template-merged, or a Drive link/id in Source Doc ID/URL).
  • DocuPDF creates the signing job and writes one-time, 7-day Sign Link - Party A/B into the row.
  • Each signer opens their link (no Google account needed), draws or types a signature.
  • When all parties have signed, the _SIGNED.pdf is saved to your signed-PDFs folder with an audit stamp (signers, timestamp, SHA-256 hash, QR).

Menu items

  • Open E-sign Engine — the sidebar listing your E-sign types.
  • Create New E-sign — open the configuration wizard directly.
  • Setup Sheet (columns) — create/map the columns DocuPDF needs (run once).
  • Enable / Disable Auto-sync — turn the scheduled scanning on or off.
  • Auto-sync Status — see whether scanning is active.
  • View Audit Logs — every event is recorded on the AuditLog sheet.

What is an E-sign type?

A signing workflow for a group of rows — e.g. MOU, Invoice, PO. One sheet can hold many types, each with its own rows, signers and schedule. The wizard detects the source mode automatically:

  • Template-based signing — sheet has Source PDF / Recipient Email columns. DocuPDF writes links; the system sends the email.
  • Standalone — no sheet columns. DocuPDF emails signers itself.
🔒 Data stays inside your Google Workspace. Nothing is stored externally. Each sign link is single-use and expires after 7 days.
